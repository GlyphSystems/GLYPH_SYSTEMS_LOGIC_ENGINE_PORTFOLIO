What the run established

Matched conditions: same arrivals, service model, physical capacity ceiling, queue limit, idempotency capability, visible-state source, stress schedule, and frozen accounting.
Stress schedule: 30 planned levels, 118 seconds each after calibration, increasing by four percentage points per level.
Recovery rule: full recovery required error rate, throughput, latency, and backlog to return to calibrated acceptable ranges for the required stable interval.

The 100% stress reference is a benchmark reference point, not a literal claim of production utilization.
